Who pays

Who pays, and who gets paid

Pays: the agent operator

Gives an AI agent a Mezo wallet and sets its spend limits, which the agent cannot change. 0.001 MUSD per paid call, no BTC for gas.

Gets paid: the data seller

Sells Mezo reads over HTTP. Deploys the Worker with its own payee address. Each paid call is one MUSD transfer to it; this toolkit takes no cut.

Not there yet: outside users, outside payers (0), revenue, anything beyond testnet. Paid use so far: 51 payments, 0.051 MUSD, all from our own wallet, up to 2026-11-12.
